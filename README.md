# AntimiPocket

A personalized pocket money and financial literacy application designed to help teenagers develop healthy spending and saving habits.

## About the Project

AntimiPocket aims to help young individuals manage their pocket money, track their spending, build savings habits, and understand their financial decisions through a personalized financial diary.

The application will also explore parental supervision and approval for selected financial activities, encouraging responsible money management while giving teenagers room to develop financial independence.

## Core Features

- **Pocket Money Management:** Track available pocket money and spending.
- **Financial Diary:** Record expenses, spending reasons, and personal reflections.
- **Savings Goals:** Set savings targets and monitor progress.
- **Parental Supervision:** Support parental involvement and approval workflows.
- **Financial Literacy:** Encourage responsible spending, budgeting, and saving.
- **Personalized Insights:** Identify spending patterns and encourage healthier financial habits.

## Architecture

The project follows a layered architecture to promote separation of concerns, maintainability, and scalability.

Planned layers include:

- **Controller Layer:** Handles HTTP requests and responses.
- **Service Layer:** Contains business logic.
- **Repository Layer:** Manages data access and persistence.
- **Model/Entity Layer:** Represents application data and domain entities.

## Vision

To help young people develop a healthier relationship with money by making financial awareness, responsible spending, and saving part of their everyday lives.
