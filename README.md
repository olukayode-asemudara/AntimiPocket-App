# AntimiPocket

**A personalized pocket money and financial literacy application for young individuals.**

## About the Project

AntimiPocket is a personal finance application designed to help young individuals develop healthy financial habits from an early age.

The goal is to create a platform where teenagers can manage their pocket money, track their spending, build savings habits, and understand their financial decisions through a personalized financial diary.

Unlike traditional savings applications, AntimiPocket aims to combine money management with financial awareness, while allowing parents or guardians to maintain appropriate oversight of their children's finances.

This project is currently in its early development stage, and its features and scope will evolve as development progresses.

## Core Objectives

- **Pocket Money Management:** Help young users keep track of their available money.
- **Financial Diary:** Allow users to record their expenses, spending reasons, and reflections on their financial decisions.
- **Savings Goals:** Enable users to set personal savings targets and monitor their progress.
- **Parental Supervision:** Introduce parent or guardian involvement for selected financial activities and approvals.
- **Financial Literacy:** Encourage responsible spending, budgeting, saving, and financial decision-making.
- **Personalized Insights:** Help users understand their spending patterns and develop healthier financial habits.

## Architectural Approach

AntimiPocket will follow a layered architecture to promote separation of concerns, maintainability, and scalability.

The application will progressively separate its responsibilities into layers such as:

- **Controller Layer:** Handles incoming HTTP requests and responses.
- **Service Layer:** Contains business logic and application rules.
- **Repository Layer:** Handles data access and persistence.
- **Model/Entity Layer:** Represents the application's data structures and domain entities.

Additional layers and components may be introduced as the project grows.

## Future Considerations

Potential future improvements include:

- User registration and authentication.
- Parent and teenager account relationships.
- Configurable parental approval workflows.
- Spending limits and pocket money allowances.
- Financial diary summaries and spending insights.
- Notifications for savings milestones and pending approvals.
- Integration with appropriate financial service providers, subject to regulatory and technical requirements.

## Project Vision

AntimiPocket aims to help young people develop a healthier relationship with money by making financial awareness, responsible spending, and saving part of their everyday lives.

**The vision is simple: help young people learn to manage money before they have to manage it independently.**
